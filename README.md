# Conveyors & Automation

***

![](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/1691181/description_a1e52798-3555-4eb1-ac3b-90171c44479f.png)

Automate item transportation, crafting and interactions using conveyor belts, mechanical arms and automated machines.

***

### **Features:**

*   **Conveyor Belts:** 3 speed tiers (Standard, Fast, Express). Each belt has two independent lanes (Left & Right).
*   **Underground Belts:** Transports items beneath terrain up to 6, 8, or 10 blocks (Standard, Fast, Express).
*   **Vertical Conveyor Belts:** Transports items straight up or down. Direction can be reversed with a Wrench.
*   **Robotic Inserters:** Animated mechanical arms that move items between chests, belts, and furnaces. Includes Burner, Standard, Fast, Long-Handed and Stack inserters. Supports item filtering (except burner inserter), and lane selection. Works with solid fuel.
*   **Splitters & Funnels:** Belt splitters with 50/50 balancing, and funnels to load/unload chests and machines directly.
*   **Filter Splitters:** Advanced splitters to set item filters for left/right outputs and configure output lane priority.
*   **Auto Crafter:** Mechanical crafting table that crafts recipes using solid fuel.
*   **Auto Slasher:** Automated mob grinder. Works with solid fuel.
*   **Crusher:** Pulverizes blocks and materials into finer resources and bonus byproducts. Works with solid fuel.
*   **Conveyor Smelter:** In-line heating tunnel that smelts items directly as they move across conveyor lines using solid fuel.
*   **Packer & Unpacker:** Packer compresses bulk items into portable Shipping Crates to dramatically multiply belt throughput. Unpacker unpacks them back into belts.
*   **Item Vacuum:** Suction block with a 5-block radius that draws in dropped world items and loads them into rear conveyors or chests.
*   **Industrial Silo:** Bulk storage with an active visual fill level gauge.
